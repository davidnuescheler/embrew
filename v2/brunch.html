Why Brunch Here

Salt Lake City brunch,
with a canyon around it.

Most brunch in Salt Lake City happens downtown. Ours happens fifteen minutes east of it, up Emigration Canyon Road, in a renovated 1960 building with a creek running past the lower patio and the canyon walls rising on either side.

Brunch is served Saturday and Sunday from 10:00am to 2:00pm. When the season allows, the creekside lower patio and the treehouse-like upper patio are open — the same wood-fired kitchen, with the canyon instead of a dining room wall. Reservations are highly recommended at weekends.

Getting Here

Fifteen minutes
from downtown.

From downtown Salt Lake City, head east on 800 South, continue onto Emigration Canyon Road and follow it up past the creek. We are at 4170 Emigration Canyon Rd, Salt Lake City, UT 84108, with parking on site — just past Ruth’s Diner and a few minutes beyond Hogle Zoo.

Patio seating is seasonal and weather dependent. In autumn the canyon turns; in winter, check road conditions before you set out.
