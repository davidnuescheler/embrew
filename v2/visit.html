Getting Here

Up the canyon,
past the creek.

We are about fifteen minutes from downtown Salt Lake City. Head east on 800 South, continue onto Emigration Canyon Road, and follow it up past the creek. Parking is on site.

The canyon road is beautiful in every season, but it does get weather. In winter, check conditions before you set out — and note that patio seating is seasonal.
