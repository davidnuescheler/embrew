The Spaces

Three distinct rooms,
one canyon.

We offer several incredible rental spaces for you and your guests to experience an exclusive event. Our remodelled building, originally built in 1960, features three distinctly different dining areas. Rental fee pricing varies depending on event date and area rented.

Downstairs Dining Room

An intimate yet lively space to hold a casual event. Attached to our open-concept pizza kitchen and bar, perfect for a small seated dinner or a lively casual event — with the ability to watch our staff in action. Tables and chairs are moveable.

Upstairs Dining Hall

What We Offer

Curated menus,
curated pours.

Whether it is a wedding related event, rehearsal dinner, reception, ceremony, business meal, holiday dinner party, or simply a celebration with family and friends, we offer curated private event experiences to remember. With unique dining areas, curated set food menus and incredible beverage options, we can cater to whatever you may be envisioning for your special occasion.

Set menus can be offered as a full-plated meal, or can be “family” style. Our wood-fired ovens, seasonal produce and local drink partnerships come with the room.
